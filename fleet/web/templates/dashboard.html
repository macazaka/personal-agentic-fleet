⚡ Antigravity Step Climbing — The Post-Meal Glucose Siphon:
• Timing: Exactly 1 hour and 45 minutes after starting each meal (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner).
• Protocol: Climb up and down 8 to 10 stairs repeatedly for 5 to 7 minutes until you feel breathless.
• Rules: Do NOT climb continuously across multiple floors. Keep to 8-10 steps and turn around.
• Testing: Rest 5 minutes until breathing normalizes, drink water, wash and dry hands, then check blood sugar!
💨 Nitric Oxide Dump (NOD) — Step Climbing Alternative

If stairs are unavailable or you have joint discomfort, perform 3 rounds of the 4 NOD movements (Deep squats, Arm raises, Non-jumping jacks, Shoulder punches). Takes only 2 minutes and releases massive nitric oxide into circulation!

🚨 Hypoglycemia Home Self-Management Protocol
• BSL 80 - 100 mg/dL: Have 1 date/khajoor + 1 tbsp fresh coconut + water.
• BSL 70 - 80 mg/dL: Have 2 dates + 1 tbsp coconut (or 2 almonds) + water.
• BSL < 70 mg/dL: Have 3 tsp sugar OR 3 dates immediately + water.
• Recheck BSL every 20 minutes until normalized!

Freedom From Diabetes — Phase 1.1 Diet Protocol
3-Meal Rapid Fat Loss • Target: 93.5 kg • HTP 138
🌟 The Golden "Rule of 4th Morsel":

The first 3 morsels of every meal MUST be 1. Salad, 2. Cooked Vegetable, and 3. Dal / Pulses in that order. Grain (rotis/millets/rice) should only be taken from the 4th morsel onwards!

⏰ Daily Meal Schedule

06:00 - 07:30 AM: Smoothie (250ml) — Green Smoothie or White Smoothie on empty stomach. Drink sip by sip over 3 mins.
08:00 - 09:00 AM: Breakfast (25-25-50% Formula) — 25% Salads (1 katori) + 25% Raw Sprouts (1 katori) + 50% Cooked Dal item (Besan chilla, moong/urad dosa, pesaruttu) + 2 tbsp Chutney.
01:00 - 02:00 PM: Lunch (25% Quad Formula) — 25% Salad (1 katori) + 25% Cooked Veg (1 katori) + 25% Dal (1 portion) + 25% Whole Grain (Khapli wheat / bajra / jowar / brown rice).
04:00 - 04:30 PM: Snack — 250ml Smoothie + 5-6 soaked almonds + 4-5 soaked walnuts + 4 tsp mixed seeds (pumpkin, flax, sunflower, watermelon).
07:00 - 08:00 PM: Dinner — Finish before 8:30 PM (definitely before 9 PM). Low-carb dal and vegetables. Sleep by 11:00 PM latest!

🥤 Smoothie Recipes (500ml batch)

Green Smoothie Recipe:
  • 1.5 cup Major Greens (Amaranth, Lettuce, Kale, or Spinach max 2x/wk)
  • 0.5 cup Minor Greens (Mint, Curry leaves, Tulsi, Coriander)
  • 1.5 cup Fruit (Apple, Berries, Guava, Kiwi, Pear)
  • 0.5 Lemon juice + 0.5 tsp Rock Salt
  • Pinch: Cinnamon, Methi, Haldi, Black Pepper powder
  • 250ml Water. Blend 3 mins.
White Smoothie Recipe:
  • 1.5 cup Bottle Gourd (check non-bitter), Cucumber, or Ash Gourd
  • 0.5 cup Minor Greens (Mint, Tulsi, Celery, Parsley)
  • 1.5 cup Fruit (Apple, Berries, Dragon Fruit, Kiwi)
  • 0.5 Lemon juice + 0.5 tsp Rock Salt
  • Pinch: Cinnamon, Methi, Haldi, Black Pepper powder
  • 250ml Water. Blend 3 mins.
🚫 Strictly Avoided Foods: Table sugar, jaggery, honey, sweets, sodas, high-GI grains (maida, white rice, cornflakes), animal milk (cow A1/A2, buffalo) & all dairy products, deep-fried snacks, and refined oils.
