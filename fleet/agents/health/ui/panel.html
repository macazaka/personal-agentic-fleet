⚡ Antigravity Step Climbing — The Post-Meal Glucose Siphon:
• Timing: Exactly 1 hour and 45 minutes after starting each meal (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner).
• Protocol: Climb up and down 8 to 10 stairs repeatedly for 5 to 7 minutes until you feel breathless.
• Rules: Do NOT climb continuously across multiple floors. Keep to 8-10 steps and turn around.
• Testing: Rest 5 minutes until breathing normalizes, drink water, wash and dry hands, then check blood sugar!
💨 Nitric Oxide Dump (NOD) — Step Climbing Alternative

If stairs are unavailable or you have joint discomfort, perform 3 rounds of the 4 NOD movements (Deep squats, Arm raises, Non-jumping jacks, Shoulder punches). Takes only 2 minutes and releases massive nitric oxide into circulation!

🚨 Hypoglycemia Home Self-Management Protocol
• BSL 80 - 100 mg/dL: Have 1 date/khajoor + 1 tbsp fresh coconut + water.
• BSL 70 - 80 mg/dL: Have 2 dates + 1 tbsp coconut (or 2 almonds) + water.
• BSL < 70 mg/dL: Have 3 tsp sugar OR 3 dates immediately + water.
• Recheck BSL every 20 minutes until normalized!
